## Round 1: Who you are

**1.1** If an investor, a client, and a mentee each read your homepage for 30 seconds, what should each walk away believing about you? One sentence each.

> 

**1.2** How do you explain what you do at a family dinner (not a pitch)?

> 

**1.3** Why do you build? Tell a real moment or story, not a principle.

> 

**1.4** What are you trying to make true in 10 years, for yourself and for others?

> 

**1.5** What do you believe that most engineers or founders around you don't?

> 

**1.6** What do people who've worked with you say you're like, good and bad?

> 

**1.7** Outside work, what do you actually do? What of it is fine to be public? (The site now says sailing and windsurfing.)

> 

**1.8** Background (where you're from, languages, family business): how much is public, what's off-limits?

> 

**1.9** Rank the audiences: partners, clients, investors, mentees. Anyone missing? Anyone you'd rather NOT attract?

> 

**1.10** Name 2–3 personal sites or people whose online presence you admire, and what specifically you like about each.

> 

**1.11** Paste 2–3 things you've written that sound most like you: an email, a LinkedIn post, a message to a friend about an idea. Any language.
